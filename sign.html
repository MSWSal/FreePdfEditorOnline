Free PDF signature tool — no account needed

Signing a PDF usually means uploading it to a website that asks you to create an account or pay for a subscription. This tool skips all of that — it signs your PDF entirely inside your browser using JavaScript, so the file is never uploaded anywhere.

How to sign a PDF online

  1. Drag and drop your PDF, or click "Choose PDF file."
  2. Create your signature: type your name in a handwriting-style font, draw it with your mouse or finger, or upload an image of your signature.
  3. Click "+ Add signature to PDF," then drag it, resize it, or click anywhere on a page to move it there.
  4. Click "Download signed PDF" to save the signed copy.
